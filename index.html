10
How we decide what to sell you

We buy on evidence, not on rankings.

Every claim we make about any product carries one of three tags. The tag tells you exactly how much weight it can take.

PROVEN

Confirmed by someone with no money in the game. A test lab such as MITRE, AV-Comparatives, AV-TEST or SE Labs, a regulator, a court, or a documented news report.

VENDOR SAYS SO

It comes from the vendor. Their site, their datasheet, their press release. We could not find anyone independent backing it up. That does not make it untrue.

COULD NOT CONFIRM

We went looking and came up empty. That is not the same as it not existing, and we will never present it to you as though it were.

What we left out, on purpose
Vendors pay to be assessed in analyst grids. They map who is spending on analyst relations, not what stops an attack. One of the vendors we sell leads its pitch with a Gartner position. We dropped it from the evidence, exactly as we dropped everybody else’s.

Nobody in this market is unscarred

What has gone wrong for each of them.

Same treatment for everyone, including the platform we sell most of.

CROWDSTRIKE · JUL 2024

8.5 million machines

A faulty update crashed roughly 8.5 million Windows machines worldwide. Over 5,000 flights grounded, banks and hospitals down. Delta put its own cost at USD 550m. Nobody broke in: they did this to themselves through their own release process.

MICROSOFT · 2023

Storm-0558

State backed hackers used a stolen signing key to read US government email. The US Cyber Safety Review Board called it a cascade of avoidable errors and said their security culture was inadequate. Those are a review board’s words, not ours.

SOPHOS · 2019 TO 2024

81,000 devices

State linked actors spent five years exploiting flaws in Sophos firewalls. The US Department of Justice indicted a Chinese national over it in December 2024. This was their own product line, not a supplier, which is harder to shrug off.

SENTINELONE · 2024 TO 2025

70 companies targeted

Attackers compromised a former logistics supplier and used it to target SentinelOne and around 70 other companies. North Korean operatives also submitted over 1,000 fake job applications. SentinelOne says their own product caught it, unverified.

BITDEFENDER · 2015

Plain text passwords

A customer and community website was breached and passwords stored in plain text were exposed. A website, not the antivirus engine. We sell more Bitdefender than anything else and we are still telling you this.

XCITIUM · NOTHING FOUND

A clean record, or fewer eyes

Ordinary vulnerabilities appear in the usual places, including a denial of service flaw in the client, but nothing on the scale of the other five. Could go either way, and public information will not settle it.

If a vendor tells you they have never had an incident, they are either very new or not telling you everything.
